The operational gap

A powerful NGFW can still deliver weak protection.

Many organisations invest heavily in Next-Generation Firewalls, yet the day-to-day operation determines how much of that investment actually becomes protection.

“An NGFW is not a router with an expensive licence. Security value depends on selecting, enabling and operating the capabilities your environment needs.”
01

Policy drift and misconfiguration

Rules, objects, profiles and exceptions accumulate. Without disciplined review, operational debt can become security exposure.

02

Dormant security capabilities

Application, identity, inspection, threat prevention and data protection capabilities may exist without being effectively used.

03

Visibility without action

Logs and alerts have limited value when teams lack the processes and skills to interpret them, prioritise them and respond consistently.

04

Remediation that does not stick

Fixing today's configuration is only part of the job. Sustainable security requires repeatable operating practices and competent operators.
